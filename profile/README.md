# Ayuuto

Ayuuto (also written ayuto) is a traditional system of collective saving and mutual financial support. A group of people regularly contributes money to a shared pool, and one member receives the entire pool each round. The process continues until everyone has received a payout.

Ayuuto is much more than a way to save money. It is also a system of trust, mutual responsibility, informal credit, community solidarity and financial self-reliance. It has helped East African families, particularly Somali women, access lump sums of money without relying on conventional bank loans. It is practised in East Africa, especially in Somalia, and throughout the East African diaspora communities living aboard in say, the United Kingdom.
